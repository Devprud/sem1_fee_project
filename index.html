Kirchhoff’s Laws:

Kirchhoff’s Laws are two fundamental rules used to analyze current and voltage in electrical circuits.

Kirchhoff’s Current Law

The total electrical current entering a circuit node (or junction) must equal the total current leaving it.

ΣI = 0

Kirchhoff’s Voltage Law

The directed sum of all electrical potential differences (voltages) around any closed loop in a circuit is zero.

ΣV = 0
